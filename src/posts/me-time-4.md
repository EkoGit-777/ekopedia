# I Finally Had a Chance to Attend a Detective Conan Movie Fan Screening!

CGV often holds special premieres for anime movies called Fan Screenings. It's usually a one-time schedule where you can watch the movie earlier than its regular release. The ticket is usually quite expensive compared to even the most expensive studio available.

That's because, at these events, you don't just get a ticket—you also get some special merchandise. Some of the items aren't even sold separately. These special events are often held at [CGV Grand Indonesia](https://www.cgv.id/en/schedule/cinema/002).

Attending one of these events has always been one of the items on my bucket list since I started working in Jakarta. It's also one of the reasons why I chose Jakarta—there are so many events held here.

And finally, yesterday, it actually happened!

I'm so happy, not only because I finally had the chance to attend a Fan Screening, but also because it was for a Detective Conan movie, which is my favorite anime.

<EmbedContainer movieId="tt39123115" />

I arrived at CGV two hours early. I was so excited that I could finally experience this event. When I arrived, there weren't many people at CGV yet, as usual. The movie started at 2 PM, so I decided to have lunch first at KFC, one floor below.

After that, I decided to wait inside CGV while reading manga.

At around 1 PM, I could see many more people starting to arrive. I knew they were going to watch the same movie as me because some of them were cosplaying as Conan characters.

It felt like I was attending a cosplay event, but on a smaller scale.

![](https://cdn.ekopedia.id/images/post/detective-conan-standee.webp)

The studio used for this event was [Starium](https://www.cgv.id/en/special_audi/detail/SA202208310229022062). It's a studio with a bigger screen, 16-channel surround sound, and a wider viewing angle. It's probably one of the best studios for movies with great sound effects. Live concerts are also often held in that studio.

The movie was really great.

For anyone who follows the Detective Conan movies, you'll know that they often have themes involving either the Detective Boys, Kaito Kid, the Black Organization, or the police.

The Detective Boys are the amateur child detective group formed by Ayumi Yoshida, Mitsuhiko Tsuburaya, and Genta Kojima.

This movie falls somewhere between a Detective Boys story and a police-related story, but I think it's more focused on the police.

The action scenes were great! I'm definitely going to watch this movie again later in 4DX. There are several pursuit scenes in the movie, and I think it would be awesome to experience them in a 4DX chair.

![](https://cdn.ekopedia.id/images/post/detective-conan-movie-29-merchandise.webp)

As I mentioned before, the Fan Screening ticket included some merchandise.

It has already been announced that the postcard can also be obtained through a stamp rally event. As for the collectible tickets, I think they will be available later. I'm not sure whether they will be sold as a set or if you'll get one random ticket per purchase.

So, the only truly special merchandise from this event was the T-shirt and the metallic badge.

I was really happy to finally attend this event. I will definitely go to another one if I get the chance—depending on what anime movie it is. :wink:
